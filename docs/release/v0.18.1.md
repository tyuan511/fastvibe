### Improved

- **The official connection is much faster when the phone is nearby.** On a direct connection, frames are no longer compressed, and a large transcript is sent in bigger pieces, so opening a chat and streaming a reply no longer pays for a slow link it is not on. A relayed connection is unchanged.
- **Connecting is quicker.** The connection starts pairing as soon as this computer has found its own public address, instead of waiting for every address to be gathered. A phone on another network still connects the same way, directly or through the relay.
- **Settings → Remote access keeps the two connections apart.** The account connection lists only phones connected through your account, each marked as such and as direct or relayed. The local network lists only devices that logged in with the LAN password, and its connected count no longer includes phones on the account connection.
- **The turn rail points at the turn you are reading.** Its highlight follows the transcript's own positions, so a long conversation no longer lights up a turn above the one on screen.
- **"Processed" counts from when you sent the message.** Reopening a chat while a reply is still running, or sending a message that waited in the queue, no longer resets the timer to zero.
