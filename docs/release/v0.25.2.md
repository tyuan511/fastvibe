### Added

- Vivi's chat now deals with a failed turn where you saw it: the error is shown in the conversation with a **Retry** action that resumes that request in its original conversation, so work already produced stays where it is instead of starting over.
- Type `/clear` in Vivi's composer to clear the main chat history while keeping her context and memory.

### Improved

- The main Vivi chat reads like a messenger conversation now: your messages sit on the right, hers on the left, consecutive messages group by sender, and a quiet time separator marks the first message of a day and any gap of five minutes or more — today, yesterday, month and day, or the year, named in your own language.
- Your own messages are no longer clamped to two lines or pinned to the top as you scroll, and the turn-by-turn side rail is gone. Attachments, Markdown replies, copying and file previews behave exactly as before.
- Your message bubbles keep one fixed blue in both light and dark appearance, independent of the selected theme's accent, so Vivi's chat looks the same across all 24 themes. The progress lines she writes are no longer drawn dimmed.
