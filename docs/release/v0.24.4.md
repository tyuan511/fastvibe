### Improved

- Running tools keep their own icons, with the existing shimmering label indicating progress. Agent actions also keep their icon while running.
- Finished runs keep agent actions, generated files, and file-change cards together inside the process fold. Expand “took …” to review them alongside the operations that produced them.
- Conversation hover previews use compact K/M notation for large token counts, making usage easier to scan.
- Vivi now shows the final reply after a task completes successfully. Clicking the notice opens the conversation that produced it; progress messages, waiting prompts, errors, and idle chatter no longer appear as notices.

### Fixed

- Fixed Vivi jumping when a notice appeared, changed size, or switched sides near a screen edge. The pet stays anchored during dragging, throwing, and landing.
- Vivi notices wait until the landing animation settles before appearing. Their five-second display timer pauses during movement, including when a task completes while Vivi is moving.
- Prevented delayed transcript reads from showing an older completion after a newer task finishes or a conversation starts running again.
