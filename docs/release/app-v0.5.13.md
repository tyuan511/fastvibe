### Fixed

- Phones now register with the account under their real name: the saved custom name, else the system device name, else the model — instead of the generic "FastVibe Android" / "FastVibe Mobile" defaults. Device lists on your computers can now tell phones apart, and renaming a phone registers reliably.
- The computers list now only offers desktop systems (macOS, Windows, Linux). Other phones and tablets signed in to the same account no longer appear as computers to connect to.
