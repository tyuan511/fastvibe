### Added

- **Sign in and your computers appear.** Sign in to your FastVibe account and every computer signed in to the same account shows up in your device list, with whether it is online. Tap one to connect — there is no address to type and no password. If the connection needs you to sign in, the app asks for that instead of showing a password form.
- **Account and settings.** Settings shows who is signed in, and signing out is there too.

### Improved

- Chats open much faster, especially over a relayed connection. A tool call is one line saying what the agent did and to what, and the app no longer downloads the full arguments and output behind it.
- Connections recover better when the network changes, and a computer that cannot be reached directly connects through FastVibe's relay instead of failing.

Update your FastVibe host to **v0.18.0 or newer** for account discovery and for the faster chats.

This Android APK is signed with FastVibe's release key. Its SHA-256 checksum is included with the download. The iOS build is delivered through TestFlight.
