### Improved

- **Follow dynamic subagent work from your phone.** A compact progress row opens a task tree, with coordinator and child relationships and blocked or failed tasks shown prominently. Open a task to read its brief, dependencies, acceptance evidence, results or execution history, and stop, retry or resume work.
- **Keep task state through reconnects.** Cached task progress stays visible while the phone reconnects, and refreshed snapshots cannot overwrite newer task updates or bring deleted tasks back.
- **More reliable connections.** The app checks quiet connections, probes after returning to the foreground or changing networks, and restores its subscriptions after reconnecting. Requests with an uncertain outcome are not automatically repeated.
- Connection diagnostics can be copied from Settings → About to help investigate disconnects.

Update your FastVibe host to **v0.17.1 or newer** to use the complete task views and result pages.

This Android APK is signed with FastVibe's release key. Its SHA-256 checksum is included with the download. The iOS build is delivered through TestFlight.
