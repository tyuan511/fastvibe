### Added

- Vivi now works on persistent conversations. Every topic you hand her keeps its own conversation: follow-up instructions, answers, and recoveries continue in the same thread instead of starting over, and the transcript survives restarts. The side pane's Vivi page shows what is in progress, recent tasks, and the questions waiting on you.
- Vivi now speaks up only when it matters: a pending question or a failed, blocked, or uncertain result raises a system notification that opens the exact conversation. Routine progress stays quiet.

### Fixed

- Connected devices in remote access now show the real device names registered in your FastVibe account instead of raw iroh endpoint ids, matching what the updated phone apps report.
- Long pasted text in messages now folds into a two-line attachment chip with the full text one click away, instead of stretching the transcript.
